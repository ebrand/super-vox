Library

    Export here and import on another server (say, from your own computer to the live game). Importing one object opens it to look over and save; importing several saves them, leaving any already here alone.

    One library for every world: saved objects can be placed (creative) or made (survival, by their recipe) in all of them.

    click: use the tool · shift-click: erase · Line: drag a row or column · Box: drag its base, let go, move up or down, click (Esc: stop) · shift: lines and boxes clear · Circle, Dome, Sphere: press the middle, drag out the radius, let go (Hollow: rings and shells) · Select: drag a box the same way, then arrows (shift ↑ ↓: up, down) or the buttons move what's in it (Esc: done) · plane: [ ] up and down, V turn, Home floor · alt-click: pick up a material · drag: turn · right-drag: move · wheel: zoom · 1-5: voxel size · the arrow marks its front (toward whoever places it)
